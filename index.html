FAQ

Good to know.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief: what the video is for, who it's for, where it will be posted, and any references you like. If you have raw footage, send it over. If not, we plan a shoot.

Can you edit footage I've already shot?

Yes. Send your raw clips and I'll handle the edit, colour, sound and graphics. I can also work with your existing brand guidelines.

How long does a project take?

It depends on length and complexity. A short reel is much quicker than a brand film. Tell me your deadline and I'll tell you honestly what's realistic.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are agreed upfront so there are no surprises. The goal is a result you're happy with, and clear feedback gets us there fast.

What formats will I receive?

Files exported for the platform you're posting on: horizontal for YouTube, vertical for Reels and Shorts, plus any other cut-downs you need.

How do we get in touch?

WhatsApp is fastest. You can also email or message me on Instagram. Details are just below.
